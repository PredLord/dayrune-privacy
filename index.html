Dayrune Privacy Policy

Privacy information for Dayrune: Habit Tracker.

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Short version

Dayrune is local-first. You can use the app without creating an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, Dayrune can securely sync your habit data to Firebase so it can be restored and kept in sync across your devices. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use your Dayrune data for advertising.

Optional Google sign-in Local-first Cloud sync when signed in No ads

1. Information Dayrune handles

When you use Dayrune without signing in

Your habits, schedules, reminders, completion history, preferences, and other app content are stored locally on your device. Dayrune does not require an account for core habit tracking.

When you choose Google sign-in and cloud sync

Dayrune uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to identify your Dayrune cloud account. Depending on the information returned by Google, this can include:

  • your Google account email address;
  • your display name;
  • a Firebase Authentication user ID used to separate your cloud data from other users.

When cloud sync is active, Dayrune stores a cloud copy of app data needed for synchronization and recovery, including:

  • habit names and habit configuration;
  • habit icons/emoji and selected colors;
  • frequency, schedule, start/end dates, and ordering information;
  • reminder schedules and settings;
  • habit completion history;
  • technical sync metadata needed to keep devices synchronized safely.

Technical information processed by Firebase

Firebase services may process limited technical information needed to provide authentication, security, and cloud functionality, such as IP address, app/device metadata, Firebase app or installation identifiers, and user-agent information.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above only to provide and operate Dayrune features, including:

  • signing you in to your optional Dayrune cloud account;
  • backing up and restoring your Dayrune data;
  • synchronizing data between your signed-in devices;
  • preventing accidental cloud overwrites and resolving synchronization state;
  • maintaining authentication, reliability, and security.

3. Third-party services

Dayrune uses Google services to provide optional account and cloud functionality:

  • Google Sign-In for user authentication;
  • Firebase Authentication for account identity and authentication;
  • Cloud Firestore for optional cloud backup and synchronization.

These services are operated by Google and are subject to Google's privacy practices. You can review Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

4. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use Dayrune habit data for targeted advertising, and Dayrune does not contain third-party advertising SDKs.

Information is transmitted to Google/Firebase only as needed to provide the optional authentication and cloud-sync functionality described in this policy.

5. Data retention and account deletion

If you do not sign in, Dayrune data remains local to your device and can be removed by deleting the data in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling Dayrune.

If you sign in, your cloud data is retained while your Dayrune cloud account remains active. You can delete your cloud account from Dayrune → Settings → Account & sync → Delete cloud account. This deletes your Dayrune Firebase Authentication account and the Dayrune cloud data associated with that account.

Local data after cloud-account deletion: deleting your Dayrune cloud account does not automatically erase habit data that is already stored locally on your device. That local data remains under your control and can be removed from the device separately.

If you no longer have access to the app, you can use our external account deletion page to request deletion.

Google/Firebase may retain limited technical records for security, fraud prevention, legal, or service-integrity purposes in accordance with their own policies and applicable law.

6. Security

Dayrune uses Firebase Authentication and per-user Cloud Firestore access controls so signed-in users can access only the cloud data associated with their own account. Data sent between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

7. Your choices

  • You can use Dayrune without signing in.
  • You can sign out at any time.
  • You can delete your Dayrune cloud account and associated cloud data.
  • You can export Dayrune data using the app's available export/backup features.

8. Children's privacy

Dayrune is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children. If we learn that personal information has been collected in a way that requires deletion under applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Dayrune's features, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
