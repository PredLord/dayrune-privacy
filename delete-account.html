Delete your Dayrune cloud account

Use the in-app deletion option whenever possible. If you no longer have access to Dayrune, you can request deletion by email.

Delete directly in Dayrune

1. Open Dayrune.
2. Go to Settings → Account & sync.
3. Tap Delete cloud account.
4. Confirm your Google identity when prompted.
5. Confirm deletion.

This deletes your Dayrune Firebase Authentication account and the Dayrune cloud data associated with it.

If you cannot access the app

Email us from, or identify, the Google email address you used to sign in to Dayrune.

Request account deletion

Email: studiothecoco@gmail.com

What we delete

  • Your Dayrune Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your Dayrune Cloud Firestore data associated with that account, including cloud-synced habits, reminder settings, completion history, and sync metadata.

What is not automatically deleted

  • Habit data that is stored only on your own device. We do not have remote access to that local copy.
  • Backup/export files that you created and saved yourself.

To remove local-only Dayrune data, delete it within Dayrune, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

Verification and security

We may ask you to verify that you control the Google account connected to the Dayrune account before processing an external deletion request. We will never ask for your Google password.

Retention

After account deletion, active Dayrune cloud records associated with the account are deleted. Google/Firebase may retain limited technical records for security, fraud prevention, legal, or service-integrity purposes according to their policies and applicable law.
